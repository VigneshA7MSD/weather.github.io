Privacy Policy for TiMp3Player

Last Updated: March 2026

TiMp3Player respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

1. Introduction

TiMp3Player is a music player application that allows users to play music from their device storage and stream music from cloud storage. We aim to provide a secure and private music listening experience.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

TiMp3Player does not collect personally identifiable information such as name, address, or phone number.

2.2 Non-Personal Information

The app may collect non-personal information such as device type, operating system version, and app performance analytics to improve the application.

2.3 Device Information

The application may access certain device information required for app functionality.

3. Permissions Used by the App

3.1 Storage Permission

TiMp3Player requires access to device storage to detect and play music files stored locally on your device. No files are uploaded or shared without user action.

3.2 Internet Access

Internet permission is required to stream music from cloud storage and retrieve music metadata.

4. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used to:

  • Provide music playback services
  • Improve app performance
  • Enable cloud streaming features
  • Maintain application security

5. Cloud Music Streaming

TiMp3Player allows users to stream music from cloud storage services. These services may store music files and provide streaming access. The app itself does not store personal user data on external servers.

6. Third-Party Services

TiMp3Player may use third-party services to support functionality such as cloud storage and backend services. These third-party services may collect limited information as governed by their own privacy policies.

7. Data Storage and Security

We prioritize protecting your data. The app does not store sensitive personal information. All local music files remain on your device unless you choose to upload them to cloud services.

8. User Data Protection

We implement appropriate security measures to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of information handled by the application.

9. Children’s Privacy

TiMp3Player does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes their child has provided personal information, they should contact us so we can remove such information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically for any updates. Changes become effective immediately after they are posted on this page.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Users have the right to understand how their data is used. If you have questions or concerns regarding privacy, you may contact us using the information below.
